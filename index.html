Privacy Policy – Dots & Boxes: New Era

Effective date: October 23, 2026

This policy explains what happens with your data when you play Dots & Boxes: New Era (the "app"), an Android game made by MT GamesStudio ("I", "me"). In short: the app does not collect, send or sell any personal data.

Data the app keeps on your device

To work, the game saves a few things only on your phone:

  • your settings (board size, number of players, sound, vibration, difficulty),
  • your statistics, achievements and Adventure progress,
  • a game in progress, so you can continue it later.

This data never leaves your device through the app. I have no server and cannot see it. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include it in your device backup to your Google account, under Google's own rules. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Data the app does not collect

The app has no account or sign-in. It does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, photos or any other personal information, and it does not use analytics or tracking.

Permissions

  • Vibration: short vibrations during the game. You can turn them off in the game menu.
  • Internet: requested by the app framework. The current version does not send any of your data over the internet.

Ads and purchases

The current version of the app shows no ads and offers no in-app purchases. If a future update adds them, this policy will be updated before that update is released and will explain what data the ad provider uses and how you can change your choices.

Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information when you download or update apps, as described in the Google Privacy Policy. I have no control over that.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
